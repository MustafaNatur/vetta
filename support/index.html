Common questions

My drinks don't show up on my other device.

Make sure both devices use the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Vettä in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Changes usually arrive within a minute while both devices are online.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for Vettä in Settings › Notifications, and that reminders are on in Vettä's own settings. Reminders stop for the day once you reach your goal.

How do I restore Vettä Pro?

Open Customise, pick any colour, tap Apply with Pro, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How is my daily goal suggested?

From your weight: 33 ml for each kilogram, rounded to 50 ml, between 1.5 and 4 litres. You can change the goal at any time in Settings.
