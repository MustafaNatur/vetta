Vettä

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

In short: Vettä does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

  • Your drinks: the amount and the time of each one.
  • Your daily goal and when you set it.
  • Your settings: weight used to suggest a goal, quick-add amounts, reminder schedule and the look you chose for the pool.

This data is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on for Vettä, Apple also keeps a copy in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account so it syncs between your devices. We cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

What we do not do

  • We do not run servers that receive your data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not share or sell anything, because we have nothing to share.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server of ours.

Purchases

Vettä Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we only receive confirmation from Apple that your device has access to Pro. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting a drink in the app deletes it everywhere it syncs. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Vettä and delete its data.

Children

Vettä does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
